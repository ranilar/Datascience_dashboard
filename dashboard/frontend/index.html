Disclaimer

Your data will not be stored, but merely compared to the data we already have.

The app works by calculating a wellness score for each input parameter. The scores are then added together to provide you with an overall score for your well-being index.

Finally, you are given feedback on how you score against optimal values found from scientific literature, and how you could improve your scoring. This gives you a way to develop positive habits to improve your physical well-being. You can also compare your statistics to our data, and see how you rank within each metric.
